## Why it exists

Neva started as one person's own assistant, not as a product. It ran on his machine against
his own notes for months before any of it was written down for someone else. Every rule in
here was earned: the grounding discipline exists because it once confidently invented a
person's nationality; the guards exist because a sync stayed silently wedged for five days;
the tools refuse to claim a message was sent because one of them used to say so without
checking. None of it is theory.

That is also the honest reason to trust the parts that are still rough. They are marked as
rough in the docs rather than smoothed over, because the whole product is an argument that an
assistant should say "I do not have that" instead of guessing, and it would be a strange
thing to sell while doing the opposite.
