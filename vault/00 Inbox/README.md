Quick captures land here, unsorted. Drop a thought, a link, a voice-to-text ramble, anything
you do not want to lose. Say "check the inbox" and your agent files each capture to where it
belongs. Empty (apart from this note and inbox.md) is the normal state, not a sign nothing is
happening.

inbox.md is different: it is the standing list your agent reads at every session start. Its
"Open actions" section is where anything that must not be forgotten goes, as checkbox lines.
Instinct proposal files ("Instinct promotions YYYY-MM-DD") also land in this folder; open one,
tick approve or reject on each block, and the next nightly run applies what you approved.
