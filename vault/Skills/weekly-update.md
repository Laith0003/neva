# Skill: Weekly Update

Draft the weekly review. Triggered by the owner or by the cadence nudge.

1. Gather since the last review: journal entries, completed project bullets, money journal
   deltas (via hledger), food log adherence if tracked, notable people interactions.
2. Draft to 09 Reviews/Weekly/<year>-W<week>.md: three sections, What moved / What stalled /
   Next week's one thing. Short, factual, sourced from notes; no invented sentiment.
3. Ask ONE question the notes cannot answer, then hand the draft over for editing.
4. Update GOALS.md progress bullets with dates.
